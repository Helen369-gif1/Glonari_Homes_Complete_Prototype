03 / Your monthly cost

Know What You Pay.

For an illustrative $1 million home, the brochure shows a gross Glonari Home fee of $8,333 per month. A planned $2,500 Global Connections benefit would leave $5,833 in monthly cash required before optional GPA funding. The benefit is conditional, not guaranteed.

05 / What the agreements mean

Your Home Agreement Is Not an Investment.

Your Glonari Home housing agreement and any GPA investment are separate legal relationships. GPA Class A shares represent equity in Glonari Properties, not direct ownership of the specific residence.
