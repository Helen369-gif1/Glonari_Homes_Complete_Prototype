Glonari Properties / Separate investment section

The Company Behind the Homes.

Glonari Properties™ is the proposed real estate business that acquires, owns and manages residential properties. Its investment program is distinct from the agreement to live in a Glonari Home.

← Back to Home
01 / The real estate business

More Than a Single Property.

The company is designed to manage a growing residential portfolio and use Gia-powered operations, coordinated purchasing, maintenance and resident services to improve efficiency.

Here we need verified information about the actual number of homes owned, addresses or regions, property values, debts, occupancy and operating performance. The brochure does not supply this portfolio data.
02 / GPA Class A

Invest in a Business, Not a Particular House.

GPA Class A represents equity in Glonari Properties. Buying GPA shares does not give an investor title to the home where they live—or to any other individual residence. Initial securities participation is limited to eligible accredited investors.

Here we should explain voting and economic rights, any distributions, the number of shares issued, dilution, qualification rules and the rights of Class A compared with other classes.
03 / Understanding the model

Property Value Is Only Part of the Story.

The brochure gives an illustrative $1 million residence plus a $400,000 target for additional operating or franchise value, yielding $1.4 million of potential gross economic value. The added value is a planning target requiring independently supportable earnings and valuation—not a proven result.

Here we need the valuation method, independently verified evidence of operating revenue and costs, and a clear distinction between the company’s economic model and actual marketable shareholder equity.
04 / The value of a share

What a Model Shows—and What You Can Sell.

The brochure illustrates a base NAV+ value rising from $100 to $437.83 per share over 36 months. NAV+ is a modeled economic measure, not a guaranteed return, available cash, or a price at which shares can be sold. Values may fall.

Here we should explain whether there is any real market for GPA, whether the company offers repurchase or redemption, what restrictions apply, and who determines the value of pledged shares.
05 / Financing and risk

Borrowing Is Not Income.

The brochure describes optional third-party lending or eligible share sales as possible sources of funds for housing costs. These do not reduce the Home fee. A loan creates debt, interest and repayment obligations; selling shares reduces the investment position. Credit and liquidity are not guaranteed.

Here we should show only approved lender terms and eligibility. Detailed leveraged GPA strategies belong in a separate investor disclosure, not on the family-focused Glonari Homes homepage.

Understand the Investment Before Deciding.

GPA is a securities investment with risk, not a savings account or a substitute for guaranteed homeownership.
