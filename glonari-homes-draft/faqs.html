FAQs

Good Questions Deserve Clear Answers.

The most important distinction: the November 2026 brochure describes a proposed program and illustrative model. Some practical resident rights and financial terms still require confirmation.

Living in a Glonari Home
How is this different from ordinary rent?

The brochure describes professionally managed housing with Gia concierge services and planned financial benefits. The precise legal occupancy protections and long-term contractual differences are not specified.

Here we should publish the actual occupancy agreement, renewal rights and a plain-language comparison with renting.
Does my family have to buy GPA shares to join?

The brochure describes a qualifying GPA position for its full-participation example, but does not clearly confirm a separate no-investment housing path.

Here we need a definitive answer on mandatory GPA holdings and whether non-accredited families can become residents.
Can I eventually buy the home I live in?

The November 2026 brochure does not establish a contractual right to purchase a particular home.

Here we need any buyout option, pricing rules, eligibility, and whether GPA shares could be exchanged or applied toward ownership.
What happens if Glonari Properties sells my residence?

The brochure does not provide the full housing-agreement protections for this situation.

Here we need the resident’s legal rights after a property sale, refinancing or financial distress.
Payments and benefits
What is the monthly payment?

The brochure’s illustrative $1 million home carries an $8,333 gross monthly Home fee. Its planned $2,500 Global Connections benefit would leave $5,833 in monthly cash required before optional GPA financing.

Here we should provide the actual fees and qualifying terms for each home.
Is the $2,500 benefit automatic or guaranteed?

No. The brochure says the benefit requires enforceable funded arrangements; without it, the full $8,333 is due.

Here we need to explain exactly where the benefit comes from, who qualifies and whether residents must participate.
Do my housing payments build ownership?

The brochure does not state that monthly Home fees accumulate as equity in the residence or are refunded when a family moves.

Here we need a clear five- or ten-year example of the amount paid and what, if anything, is refundable.
Gia and everyday life
What is Gia?

Gia is Glonari Intelligent Agent, a personal AI concierge designed to help coordinate services, maintenance, schedules and personal assistance according to available features and permissions.

Here we need to distinguish services operating today from future plans.
Can Gia help me earn through Global Connections?

The November brochure discusses planned economic benefits but does not explain the prior personal Home Business income model in detail.

Here we need to confirm whether additional member earnings or a separate Home Business still exist.
GPA investment
Do GPA shares make me an owner of my house?

No. GPA Class A is equity in Glonari Properties, not direct title to an individual residence.

Here we need the exact shareholder rights and legal documentation.
Can I sell my shares or borrow against them?

No market, repurchase, redemption, share price or liquidity is guaranteed. Possible borrowing is subject to lender approval, eligible collateral and restrictions.

Here we need actual exit options, approved lending arrangements and any eligibility limitations.
Who may invest in GPA?

Initial securities participation is limited to eligible accredited investors. Broader access is a future objective requiring authorization.

Here we need an explicit distinction between eligibility for GPA securities and eligibility for the Home living program.
